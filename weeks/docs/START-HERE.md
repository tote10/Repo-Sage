## Your daily rhythm (same 5 steps every single day)
1. Open **`tracker.md`** → find the first empty `[ ]` box. **That's today's job.**
2. Open the matching **`week-XX.md`** → read just **that one day**. Ignore the rest.
3. **Try it yourself first.** Let it break. Getting stuck is the point — that's the learning.
4. **Stuck for more than ~20 min?** Come to me (Claude). Paste:
   - what you're trying to do,
   - the code you wrote,
   - the exact error.
   I'll explain the *concept* and unblock you — not just hand you the answer.
5. It works? Change `[ ]` to `[x]` in `tracker.md`. **Done for today. Stop.**

---

## The 3 rules that keep you sane
- **One day at a time.** Never look at Week 5 while you're on Week 1. It's not for you yet.
- **"Working and ugly" beats "perfect and unfinished."** Always.
- **Confused is normal.** Every day is designed to confuse you *first*, then click. Push the
  wall; if it won't move in 20 min, ask me.

## When you're really lost, ask yourself only this
> "What is the **first unchecked box** in `tracker.md`?"

Go do that one thing. That's it. The whole project is just that question, 60 times.
